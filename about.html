My Story

From Humble Beginnings to Minden's Go-To Handyman

My name is Larry Alexander. I grew up here in Louisiana, and I've been working with my hands for as long as I can remember. Over 20 years ago, I started fixing things for family, friends, and neighbors and it just never stopped.

I started Jack Of All Trades because I got tired of seeing hardworking people get overcharged for sloppy work. There are too many contractors out there who cut corners or disappear halfway through a job.

"No job too big or small, and I don't get paid till you're happy."— Larry Alexander, Owner

My promise to every customer is simple: honest pricing, clean workmanship, and a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not 100% happy with the work when I'm finished, you don't pay.

Today, I serve homes and businesses throughout Minden, Shreveport, Bossier City, and the surrounding parishes.
