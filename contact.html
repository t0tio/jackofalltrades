Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are 100% free. Give me a call, tell me what you need done, and I'll give you an honest price.

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Minden, but I serve all of Webster, Bossier, and Caddo parishes including Shreveport, Bossier City, Haughton, Sibley, Doyline, and surrounding areas.

Are you available for emergency work?

Yes. I'm available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call any time.

What is your payment policy?

Simple: I don't get paid until you're happy with the work. If you're not 100% satisfied, I'll make it right before any money changes hands.

Do you do full electrical work?

I don't do full electrical panel work, but I do replace switches, outlets, light fixtures, and install ceiling fans.

Can you work with my existing security camera system?

Absolutely. I can troubleshoot, expand, or reconfigure your existing setup or install a completely new system if you prefer.
